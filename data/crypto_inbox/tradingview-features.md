Volume footprint Displays the distribution of trading volume at various price levels for each candlestick within a specified timeframe. Time price opportunity Enables traders to visualize price movements as the profile period progresses, illustrating the concentration of prices at specific levels during a given timeframe. Session volume profile Calculates volume data within a specified session or sub-session, allowing traders to analyze intraday volume activity for a particular segment of the session. Candles One of the most popular traditional charts on TradingView. Conveys the size of price movements using different colors. Bars A chart, composed of multiple price bars, each visualizing the price movement of an asset or security over a specified time period. Volume candles This chart visualizes trading volume, allowing traders to assess the volume of trades using candlesticks. Range Enables users to analyze the price movements of financial instruments with more clarity, minimizing market noise. Heikin Ashi Reduces market noise by smoothing significant price swings, making it easier to identify trend movements within the market. Renko Allows users to detect major price moves within a Renko box, if they occurred. Line break Focuses solely on price movements, ignoring time intervals within this Japanese chart style. Point & figure Illustrates price movements as a lone metric, disregarding time intervals in their creation. Kagi Utilizes vertical lines (green for up and red for down) connected by small horizontal lines to highlight significant movements in an asset's price. Area Visualizes data in a similar way to a line chart. An area chart plots and connects data with the area in between filled with color to convey information. HLC area Focused on three data-point areas — High-Low-Close (HLC) — to show how price changes over a period of time, allowing traders to disregard the open price value, which is less significant for analysis. Baseline Explores price movements relative to a selected baseline, giving traders the tools needed to analyze price fluctuations. Line Connects data points with a single line to form a classic line chart. Step line Forms a series of steps using horizontal and vertical lines to highlight the irregularity of changes within the market. Line with markers Adds markers to the classic line charts to represent essential data points. High-low Displays the range of prices from high to low on each bar. Columns The chart represents the change in the real price of an instrument through the use of columns.
